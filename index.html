    Why I love Clinton Hill/Fort Greene

    It's overwhelmingly democrat and sometimes known as 'commie corridor,' as my mother lovingly puts it. Overall, it's not the most gentrified neighborhood, but still nice enough with nice restaurants and Pratt University nearby. It's close to a few trains, like the G, A and C, which is helpful. Where I live in particular, it's quiet because my street is unpopular.
